Mohammed Barakat is an incoming PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, starting August 2026, advised by [Prof. Levent Burak Kara](https://www.meche.engineering.cmu.edu/directory/bios/kara-levent.html) in the Visual Design and Engineering Lab (VDEL).

Mohammed graduated from the University of Texas at Tyler with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering (GPA 3.95, President's List, Outstanding Student in the College of Engineering), where his work spanned computational modeling, embedded systems, and AI-driven engineering design. He served as President of the ASME student chapter and worked as an undergraduate research assistant and lab assistant in the College of Engineering.

His technical experience includes machine learning for materials science (CO₂ sequestration and hydrogen storage via molecular dynamics), ESP32/Arduino-based embedded systems, CAD and simulation in SolidWorks and ANSYS, and full-stack development. His senior capstone project was a NICU sensory bonding device integrating haptic feedback, scent delivery, and a mobile app interface. He holds certifications in Machine Learning (DeepLearning.AI / Andrew Ng) and IBM Data Science, and is an Autodesk Inventor Certified User.

Mohammed is bilingual in Arabic and English. His long-term goal is to become an AI researcher whose systems are built to survive contact with physical reality, with an eye toward roles at places like NVIDIA, national labs, or deep tech.
