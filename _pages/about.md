Mohammed Barakat is a Mechanical Engineering graduate from the University of Texas at Tyler (GPA 3.95, President's List, Outstanding Student in College of Engineering) with a research focus on applying artificial intelligence and machine learning to mechanical engineering problems. His work spans computational modeling, embedded systems, and AI-driven engineering design.

During his undergraduate career, Mohammed served as President of the ASME student chapter and worked as a lab assistant in the College of Engineering. His senior capstone project involved developing a NICU sensory bonding device integrating haptic feedback, scent delivery, and a mobile app interface — a project that combined hardware design with software development.

His technical experience includes machine learning for materials science applications (CO₂ sequestration and hydrogen storage), ESP32-based embedded systems, CAD and simulation in SolidWorks and ANSYS, and mobile app development. He holds certifications in Machine Learning from Andrew Ng's specialization and IBM Data Science, and is an Autodesk Inventor Certified User.

Mohammed is bilingual in Arabic and English and is actively pursuing graduate PhD research opportunities in AI-applied mechanical engineering.
