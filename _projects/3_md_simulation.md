Completed as part of an NSF-funded Online Research Experience for Undergraduates (O-REU) at Texas A&M University, studying nanoscale deformation in silicon using molecular dynamics driven by machine-learned interatomic potentials.

**Tools:** LAMMPS, OVITO, Atomsk, Wulffpack

**Highlights:**

- Machine-learning-enhanced molecular dynamics simulation of nanoscale silicon deformation
- Nanostructure modeling and analysis of deformation mechanisms

[View the project poster](https://tinyurl.com/m7xk95z4) presented at Texas A&M University.
