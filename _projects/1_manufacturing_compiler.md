An executable SysML v2 framework that treats manufacturing systems as a graph spanning four interdependent layers: requirements, structure, fabrication, and suppliers. The project integrates AI agent orchestration, constraint propagation, and graph neural network (GNN) surrogates so that design changes propagate automatically across the layers, catching manufacturability and supply-chain conflicts before they reach the shop floor.

**Focus areas:**
- Executable systems modeling with SysML v2
- Constraint propagation across coupled design layers
- GNN surrogates for fast feasibility and cost estimation
- AI agent orchestration for design-to-fabrication workflows

This is ongoing PhD research at the Visual Design and Engineering Lab (VDEL), Carnegie Mellon University.
